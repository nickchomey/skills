`wayfinder` now describes each epic with a paragraph instead of a label, and keeps two tables in the map: every ticket by number with its epic, type and state, and the same tickets grouped under their epics, split into open, parked and resolved. It states the order of work once so an epic's position has a reason, records the calls made when charting runs without an interview, moves a resolved ticket to `issues/resolved/` with every link repointed, and parks a HITL ticket whose questions go unanswered instead of answering for the human. A worked example ships beside the skill, and the local-markdown tracker template gains the resolved folder and the matching table conventions.
